QUEST 2

Spell Workshop: Feedback Spells

+15 XP

Constructive feedback is kind, helpful, and specific advice that helps a writer build up their writing. Here are your two spell types:

Compliment Spells

  • "I loved ..."
  • "It was so cool the way you ..."
  • "My favorite part was ..."

Suggestion Spells

  • "What would happen if ..."
  • "Maybe you could make it even stronger by ..."
  • "Maybe if you tried ..."
  • "I was a little confused by ..."
  • "It would be helpful if ..."
